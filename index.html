Forge app for Jira Service Management Cloud · in development

Customers see who is on it, and when it will be done.

Portal Request View Plus adds a panel to each request in your Jira Service Management help centre: the assignee's name, how the request is tracking against its service targets, linked requests, and the read-only fields you choose. Customers get their answer on the page instead of asking "any update?".

Ask for early access How it keeps data safe

What customers can see

Only what you switch on, only on requests they can already open.

Who is working on it
The assignee's display name, or "Not assigned yet". Never an email address or account ID.
Service targets
Each SLA as On track, Due soon, Overdue, On hold, Met or Missed, with time left, a progress bar and the due time. Rename internal SLA names to ones customers understand, and choose which SLAs appear.
Linked requests
Key, summary, status and how each one relates, for example "is blocked by". Limited to the same service project unless you choose otherwise.
Read-only fields
Any field you pick, shown as text, with your own names, grouped under your own headings, in your order. Comments, worklogs, attachments, time tracking and similar internal fields can never be shown.

Set up

For Jira administrators, in Jira settings under Apps.

  1. Pick a request type

    Choose the service project and request type. Nothing is shown anywhere until you switch a request type on.

  2. Choose what shows

    Switch on the assignee, service targets, linked requests and fields, and set the names customers see.

  3. Copy it across

    Use the settings for one request type, or apply them to every request type in the project at once.

Your data stays in Atlassian

The App runs on Atlassian's Forge platform, declares no outside network access, and stores only your settings, never your requests or anyone's personal data. Every value passes an allow-list you control and a deny list you cannot override. See the privacy policy and the terms.
