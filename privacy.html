Privacy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. Applies to Portal Request View Plus before and after its Atlassian Marketplace listing.

In short: Portal Request View Plus runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. It reads request details from your Jira Service Management site only to show the ones your administrator switched on, to people who can already open that request. It stores no personal data, keeps no copy of your requests, and sends nothing outside Atlassian.

Who we are

Portal Request View Plus ("the App") is a Forge app for Jira Service Management Cloud. It is made by Rishabh Chulet, an independent developer in India trading as Creet ("the Developer", "we", "us"). The organisation that installs the App on its Atlassian site is "the Customer"; the people who use the Customer's help centre are "portal users".

What the App does

It adds a panel to request pages in the Customer's help centre. A Jira administrator chooses, per request type, which of these the panel shows: the assignee's display name, the request's service targets (SLAs), linked requests, and read-only fields the administrator selects. Nothing is shown for a request type until an administrator switches it on.

What the App reads

The App runs on Atlassian's Forge platform and reads data through Atlassian's own APIs, as the App, at the moment a panel or the admin page is opened:

  • For a request page: the request's reporter, participants and request type (to check the viewer may see it), its assignee, its issue links, its service targets, and only the fields the administrator selected for that request type.
  • For the admin page, used only by Jira administrators: the site's service projects, request types, field names and types, and issue link types, and whether the current user is a Jira administrator.

The App never reads comments, worklogs, attachments, time tracking, watchers, votes or security levels, and an administrator cannot switch them on.

What portal users see

Only what the administrator switched on, and only on requests they can already open. People appear by display name only: the App never shows email addresses, account IDs or avatars, and never sends them to the browser.

What the App stores

Only the administrator's configuration: for each request type, which items are switched on, which fields and link types are selected, and the labels and headings the administrator typed. It is kept in the App's storage on Atlassian's Forge platform for the Customer's site. It contains no personal data and no request content. The App does not copy, cache or keep requests, comments, names or field values.

Logs

To diagnose problems, the App writes short technical log entries to Atlassian's Forge logging service: the event name, the request key and request type, the kind of viewer (for example "customer" or "agent") and whether the viewer check passed, which data source answered, counts of items shown, and timings. Logs never contain field values, summaries, names, email addresses or account IDs. Atlassian keeps these logs under its Forge platform's retention rules; the Developer can read them only for the purpose of support and fixing errors.

What the App sends

Nothing outside Atlassian. The App declares no external network access, so it cannot call any server other than Atlassian's own APIs. It has no analytics, tracking, advertising or AI services of its own.

What others collect

  • Atlassian: hosts and runs the App, its storage and its logs on the Forge platform, and, once the App is listed, processes installation, billing and Marketplace accounts under its own terms and privacy policy. See Atlassian's privacy policy.
  • Email support: if you write to support@creet.in, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and use it only to answer you. Please do not send request contents or personal data of your customers unless we need them to fix a problem.

Retention and deletion

The configuration stays in the App's storage while the App is installed. When the Customer uninstalls the App, Atlassian handles the App's stored data under its retention rules for Forge apps. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to help and are deleted on request.

Children

The App is a business tool for Jira Service Management and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version of the App needs to store or send any personal data, this page will say so with a new date before that version is released.
